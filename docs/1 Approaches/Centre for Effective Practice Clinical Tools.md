*Clinical tools from the [Centre for Effective Practice](https://cep.health/) (CEP), a Canadian not-for-profit that builds point-of-care tools for primary care clinicians, mostly with Ontario partners. Each link opens the tool's page; open the tool itself with its **Access tool** button. These tools are written for Ontario: check BC Guidelines, PharmaCare coverage and BC referral routes before applying them.*

[Open CEP Clinical Tools](https://cep.health/clinical-tools-supports/clinical-tools/){ .md-button .md-button--primary target="_blank" rel="noopener" }

## Tools by System

*Tools are listed by system, with the year of their latest update. Where a BC Guideline on the same topic is more recent, it is linked beside the tool.*

<div class="nppr-filter" data-placeholder="Filter tools, e.g. pain"></div>

### Cardiovascular (1)

- [Heart Failure](https://cep.health/clinical-products/managing-patients-with-heart-failure/) (2024) — *assessment, diagnosis and management across ejection fractions, therapy titration, palliative planning and referral; EMR tool*

### Respiratory (2)

- [Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)](https://cep.health/clinical-products/chronic-obstructive-pulmonary-disease-copd/) (2024) · [BC Guideline: COPD: Diagnosis and Management (2024, revised 2025)](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/practitioner-professional-resources/bc-guidelines/copd) — *diagnosis, maintenance therapy by symptoms, exacerbations and lung function, self-management; EMR tool*
- [Seasonal Preparedness Resource](https://cep.health/clinical-products/seasonal-preparedness-resource/) (2026) — *respiratory season planning: chronic condition optimization, infant RSV prevention, immunizations, testing and IPAC (Ontario programs)*

### Endocrine and Metabolic (2)

- [Type 2 Diabetes](https://cep.health/clinical-products/type-2-diabetes/) (2023) · [BC Guideline: Diabetes Care (2021, revised May 2026)](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/practitioner-professional-resources/bc-guidelines/diabetes) — *insulin and non-insulin pharmacotherapy tools, hypoglycemia, insulin pen chart, drug pearls; EMR tool*
- [Pharmacotherapy for Obesity Management](https://cep.health/clinical-products/pharmacotherapy-for-obesity-management/) (2025) — *assessment, eligibility, readiness and cost for anti-obesity medication in adults*

### Renal and Genitourinary (1)

- [Urinary Incontinence in Women](https://cep.health/clinical-products/urinary-incontinence/) (2020) — *screening, diagnosis and a management plan built on patient preferences and access*

### Musculoskeletal (6)

- [Low Back Pain: CORE Back Tool](https://cep.health/clinical-products/low-back-pain/) (2026) — *exam for mechanical back pain syndromes, red and yellow flags, imaging and referral thresholds*
- [Neck Pain and Headache: CORE Neck Tool and Headache Navigator](https://cep.health/clinical-products/core-neck-tool-and-headache-navigator/) (2016) — *exam for mechanical neck pain with screening for other causes, plus primary headache management*
- [Osteoarthritis](https://cep.health/clinical-products/osteoarthritis/) (2017) — *hip, knee and hand osteoarthritis: non-drug and drug management, triggers for investigation or referral*
- [Chronic Non-Cancer Pain](https://cep.health/clinical-products/chronic-non-cancer-pain/) (2024) — *goal-based management plan for chronic pain, with appendices, urine drug screening and patient handouts*
- [Fibromyalgia, ME/CFS and POTS](https://cep.health/clinical-products/fm-mecfs-and-pots/) (2024) — *recognition, assessment, diagnosis and management of each, with clinician and patient resources*
- [Manual Therapy for MSK Pain](https://cep.health/clinical-products/manual-therapy/) (2020) — *education, exercise and manual therapy for MSK pain, including referral to chiropractic, physiotherapy or massage therapy*

### Neurology (2)

- [Concussion](https://cep.health/clinical-products/concussion/) (2019) · [BC Guideline: Concussion / Mild Traumatic Brain Injury (2024, revised 2025)](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/practitioner-professional-resources/bc-guidelines/concussion-mild-traumatic-brain-injury-mtbi) — *diagnosis and management of concussion in adults, with a recovery plan and monitoring appendices*
- [Dementia Care](https://cep.health/clinical-products/antipsychotics-and-dementia-primary-care-edition/) (2025) — *diagnosis of dementia and its subtypes, BPSD management, antipsychotic discussion guide for families (English and French)*

### Dermatology (1)

- [Psoriasis](https://cep.health/clinical-products/psoriasis/) (2026) — *assessment and diagnosis, including in skin of colour, and topical and systemic treatment options*

### Infectious Disease (2)

- [Early Lyme Disease](https://cep.health/clinical-products/early-lyme-disease/) (2020) — *early localized Lyme disease and tick bite management, with a patient handout (English and French)*
- [Women-Centred HIV Care](https://cep.health/clinical-products/women-centred-hiv-care/) (2020) — *care for women living with HIV, with a toolkit for patients (English and French)*

### Mental Health and Substance Use (9)

- [Anxiety and Depression](https://cep.health/clinical-products/anxiety-and-depression/) (2023) — *validated screening tools, stepped care and treatment options in adults; EMR tool*
- [ADHD in Adults](https://cep.health/clinical-products/adhd/) (2025) — *ASRS screening, DSM-5 diagnosis and management with comorbid conditions*
- [Adult Mental Health](https://cep.health/clinical-products/adult-mental-health/) (2017) — *assessing risk and functional impairment in adults, with discussion aids and samples*
- [Youth Mental Health](https://cep.health/clinical-products/youth-mental-health/) (2017) — *anxiety and depression in people aged 12 to 24 over a series of visits*
- [Chronic Insomnia](https://cep.health/clinical-products/chronic-insomnia/) (2017) — *assessment, underlying causes, and non-drug and drug options in adults*
- [Alcohol Use Disorder](https://cep.health/clinical-products/alcohol-use-disorder/) (2019) · [BC Guideline: High-Risk Drinking and Alcohol Use Disorder (2024)](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/practitioner-professional-resources/bc-guidelines/high-risk-drinking-and-alcohol-use-disorder) — *screening and pharmacotherapy, with notes for youth, older adults and pregnancy*
- [Non-Medical Cannabis](https://cep.health/clinical-products/non-medical-cannabis-resource/) (2018) — *discussing cannabis use and screening for cannabis use disorder*
- [Opioid Manager](https://cep.health/clinical-products/opioid-manager/) (2017) — *monitoring, tapering and switching opioids in chronic non-cancer pain*
- [Opioid Tapering Template](https://cep.health/clinical-products/opioid-tapering-template/) (2018) — *template for planning and adjusting an opioid taper, with a patient handout*

### Sexual and Reproductive Health (3)

- [Endometriosis](https://cep.health/clinical-products/endometriosis/) (2026) — *assessment, diagnosis, fertility considerations and long-term management*
- [Menopause Management](https://cep.health/clinical-products/menopause-management/) (2025) — *perimenopause to postmenopause, menopausal hormone therapy risk assessment and shared decisions*
- [Preconception Health Care](https://cep.health/clinical-products/preconception/) (2019) — *preconception risk assessment and risk reduction (English and French); EMR form*

### Pediatrics (1)

- [Preventing Childhood Obesity](https://cep.health/clinical-products/childhood-obesity/) (2016) — *growth measurement and healthy-living conversations for ages 2 to 17 (English and French)*

### Older Adults (4)

- [Preventive Care for Older People](https://cep.health/clinical-products/preventive-care-for-older-people/) (2026) — *screening and counselling checklist for older adults; PDF and EMR reminder tool*
- [Falls Prevention](https://cep.health/clinical-products/falls-prevention/) (2021) — *fall prevention and management in primary care, with long-term care guides and a patient handout*
- [Benzodiazepine Use in Older Adults](https://cep.health/clinical-products/benzodiazepine-use-in-older-adults/) (2019) — *starting, continuing or stopping benzodiazepines at 65 and over*
- [Proton Pump Inhibitor Use in Older Adults](https://cep.health/clinical-products/proton-pump-inhibitor-use/) (2020) — *starting, continuing or stopping PPIs at 65 and over, and alternatives*

### General and Preventive Care (2)

- [Poverty: A Clinical Tool for Primary Care](https://cep.health/clinical-products/poverty-a-clinical-tool-for-primary-care-providers/) (2016) — *screening questions on income and benefits, with links to government and community supports; choose the BC version*
- [Anal Cancer Screening for People Living with HIV](https://cep.health/clinical-products/anal-cancer-screening/) (2026) — *screening decisions in primary care, with a patient resource (Ontario program)*
