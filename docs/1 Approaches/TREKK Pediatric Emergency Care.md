*Bottom Line Recommendations, algorithms and evidence repositories from [TREKK](https://trekk.ca/) (Translating Emergency Knowledge for Kids), Canada's national network for pediatric emergency care, written for general emergency departments that see children. Each line is one topic with its year and PDF; the algorithm, medication table and evidence repository sit beside it, and the TREKK topic page adds parent resources, videos and order sets.*
