*Primary care guides from the [Centre for Addiction and Mental Health](https://www.camh.ca/) (CAMH), Canada's largest mental health teaching hospital, in Toronto. Each guide is a short chapter on recognizing, assessing and starting treatment for a mental health or substance use problem, and when to refer. They are written for Ontario's referral routes and services: check your own province's guideline, drug coverage and crisis lines before applying them elsewhere.*
