*Short "Five things to know about ..." clinical articles from [CMAJ](https://www.cmaj.ca/) (the Canadian Medical Association Journal), each a short summary of a condition, test or treatment, written for Canadian practice and free to read. Articles from 2010 onward, grouped by body system. Each was current when published: check the year, and use current BC and Canadian guidance for practice decisions.*
