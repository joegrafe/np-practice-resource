*Quality standards from [Ontario Health](https://www.ontariohealth.ca/) (formerly Health Quality Ontario): each one states what high-quality care looks like for a condition, as a short list of quality statements, with a one-page clinician placemat and a patient guide. They are written for Ontario, where they replace the role the BC Guidelines play: check your own province's guideline, drug coverage and referral routes before applying them elsewhere.*
