## How to Do It in BC

1. Apply to BCCNM and pass an approved exam ([how to apply](https://www.bccnm.ca/NP/applications_registration/how_to_apply/Pages/Default.aspx)).
2. Once licensed, apply for an MSP practitioner number with [Form 2998](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/health/forms/2998fil.pdf), and enrol in PRIME for PharmaNet ([Prescribing Authority](Prescribing%20Authority.md)).
3. Each year, finish your [Personal Practice Review](https://www.bccnm.ca/NP/QA/ppr/Pages/Default.aspx) and [renew](https://www.bccnm.ca/NP/applications_registration/how_to_renew/Pages/Default.aspx) by March 31.
