## How to Do It in BC

1. Get PharmaNet access: enrol in [PRIME](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/practitioner-professional-resources/pharmacare/pharmanet-bc-s-drug-information-network/prime/individual-enrolment-in-prime) with the BC Services Card app, then send the approval to your site's PharmaNet administrator. A private community practice must first [register the site in PRIME](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/practitioner-professional-resources/system-access/health-practice-access-to-pharmanet).
2. Order Controlled Prescription Program pads or printer sheets from your [BCCNM account](https://www.bccnm.ca/NP/learning/cpp/Pages/Default.aspx).
3. For coverage and Special Authority requests, use [Drug Coverage](../5%20Prescribing/Drug%20Coverage.md).
4. If you're unsure whether you can prescribe a drug, ask [BCCNM Standards Support](https://public.bccnm.ca/standardssupport).
