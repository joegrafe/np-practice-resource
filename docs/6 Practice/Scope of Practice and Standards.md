## How to Do It in BC

1. Find the standard for the activity in [BCCNM - Nurse Practitioners: Standards](https://www.bccnm.ca/NP/PracticeStandards/Pages/Default.aspx); most have a page of FAQs and case studies under [BCCNM learning resources](https://www.bccnm.ca/NP/learning/Pages/Default.aspx).
2. If the standards don't answer it, send the question to [BCCNM Standards Support](https://public.bccnm.ca/standardssupport).
3. For prescribing questions, start with [Prescribing Authority](Prescribing%20Authority.md).
