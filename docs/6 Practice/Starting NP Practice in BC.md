## Licence and Protection

- Pass an approved NP exam and apply to BCCNM — *[Registration and Licensure](Registration%20and%20Licensure.md)*
- Pay for CNPS liability protection at licensure, with or without NNPBC membership — *[Registration and Licensure: Liability Protection](Registration%20and%20Licensure.md#liability-protection)*
- Read the BCCNM NP standards, limits and conditions that apply to you — *[Scope of Practice and Standards](Scope%20of%20Practice%20and%20Standards.md)*

## MSP, PharmaNet and Prescribing

- Apply for an MSP practitioner number (Form 2998) — *[Registration and Licensure: MSP Practitioner Number](Registration%20and%20Licensure.md#msp-practitioner-number)*
- Set up encounter record submission (Form 2871 and Teleplan) — *[Practice Models and Compensation: Encounter Records](Practice%20Models%20and%20Compensation.md#encounter-records-and-teleplan)*
- Enrol in PRIME for PharmaNet access, and register a private community practice site if you need one — *[Prescribing Authority: PharmaNet and PRIME](Prescribing%20Authority.md#pharmanet-and-prime)*
- Order Controlled Prescription Program pads or printer sheets from your BCCNM account — *[Prescribing Authority: Controlled Drugs](Prescribing%20Authority.md#controlled-drugs-and-substances)*

## Contract and Clinic

- Find a role and contract, for example through NNPBC's NP-PIP and PCN contracts — *[Practice Models and Compensation](Practice%20Models%20and%20Compensation.md)*
- Register with WorkSafeBC if you incorporate or employ staff — *[Practice Models and Compensation: WorkSafeBC](Practice%20Models%20and%20Compensation.md#worksafebc)*
- Confirm who is the custodian of the records you create, and how privacy and virtual care are handled in your clinic — *[Documentation, Privacy and Records](Documentation%20Privacy%20and%20Records.md)*

## Ongoing

- Join a peer network or mentorship program — *[Community of Practice](Community%20of%20Practice.md)*
- Each year, finish your Personal Practice Review and renew your licence by March 31 — *[Registration and Licensure: Quality Assurance](Registration%20and%20Licensure.md#quality-assurance-and-currency)*
- Renew your PRIME enrolment every year — *[Prescribing Authority](Prescribing%20Authority.md#pharmanet-and-prime)*
