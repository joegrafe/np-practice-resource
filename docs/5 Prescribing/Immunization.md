## Key Sources

- [BCCDC - BC Immunization Manual (Communicable Disease Control Manual, Chapter 2)](https://www.bccdc.ca/health-professionals/clinical-resources/communicable-disease-control-manual/immunization) — *BC's best-practice guidelines for immunization services; updated part by part, with each change listed in BCCDC Admin Circulars*
    - [Part 1: Immunization Schedules (September 2026)](https://www.bccdc.ca/resource-gallery/Documents/Guidelines%20and%20Forms/Guidelines%20and%20Manuals/Epid/CD%20Manual/Chapter%202%20-%20Imms/Part_1_Schedules.pdf) — *routine schedules by age, minimum intervals, vaccine administration errors*
    - [Part 2: Immunization of Special Populations](https://www.bccdc.ca/health-professionals/clinical-resources/communicable-disease-control-manual/immunization/immunization-of-special-populations) — *high-risk, immunocompromised, chronic conditions, occupation and lifestyle; dated by section*
    - [Part 3: Management of Anaphylaxis in a Non-Hospital Setting (February 2025)](https://www.bccdc.ca/resource-gallery/Documents/Guidelines%20and%20Forms/Guidelines%20and%20Manuals/Epid/CD%20Manual/Chapter%202%20-%20Imms/Part_3_Anaphylaxis.pdf)
    - [Part 4: Biological Products (Vaccines & Immune Globulins)](https://www.bccdc.ca/health-professionals/clinical-resources/communicable-disease-control-manual/immunization/biological-products) — *each product's eligibility, dosing and schedule; dated by product*
    - [Part 5: Adverse Events Following Immunization (February 2025)](https://www.bccdc.ca/resource-gallery/Documents/Guidelines%20and%20Forms/Guidelines%20and%20Manuals/Epid/CD%20Manual/Chapter%202%20-%20Imms/Part_5_AEFI.pdf)
    - [Appendix B: Administration of Biological Products (June 2022)](https://www.bccdc.ca/resource-gallery/Documents/Guidelines%20and%20Forms/Guidelines%20and%20Manuals/Epid/CD%20Manual/Chapter%202%20-%20Imms/Appendix_B_Administration.pdf) — *route and site*
    - [Appendix C: Contraindications and Precautions for Immunization (July 2025)](https://www.bccdc.ca/resource-gallery/Documents/Guidelines%20and%20Forms/Guidelines%20and%20Manuals/Epid/CD%20Manual/Chapter%202%20-%20Imms/Appendix_C_ContraindicationsPrecautions.pdf)
- [Public Health Agency of Canada - Canadian Immunization Guide (updated 2026)](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/canadian-immunization-guide.html) — *national guidance in four parts: key immunization information, vaccine safety, specific populations and immunizing agents*
    - [Timing of vaccine administration](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/publications/healthy-living/canadian-immunization-guide-part-1-key-immunization-information/page-10-timing-vaccine-administration.html)
    - [Contraindications, precautions and concerns](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/publications/healthy-living/canadian-immunization-guide-part-2-vaccine-safety/page-3-contraindications-precautions-concerns.html)
    - [Immunization of persons with chronic diseases](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/publications/healthy-living/canadian-immunization-guide-part-3-vaccination-specific-populations/page-7-immunization-persons-with-chronic-diseases.html)
- [Public Health Agency of Canada - National Advisory Committee on Immunization (NACI)](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/immunization/national-advisory-committee-on-immunization-naci.html) — *NACI statements and recommendations, which the Canadian Immunization Guide and BC programs draw on; each statement is dated*

## BC Program Updates

- [BCCDC - Immunization program updates and Q&As](https://www.bccdc.ca/health-professionals/clinical-resources/immunization) — *health care provider Q&As for new and changed BC programs, each dated, such as the older adult RSV program (September 2026), infant RSV program (updated August 2026), HPV program (updated March 2026) and PCV20 (July 2025)*

## Adverse Events and Anaphylaxis

- [BCCDC - Adverse Events Following Immunization (AEFI)](https://www.bccdc.ca/health-professionals/clinical-resources/adverse-events-following-immunization) · [Report form (PDF)](https://www.bccdc.ca/resource-gallery/Documents/Guidelines%20and%20Forms/Forms/Immunization/Vaccine%20Info/AEFI-case-report-form-short.pdf) — *how to report an adverse event to your local health unit, with the short report form for providers outside public health and the anaphylaxis worksheet*

## Vaccine Supply and Handling

- [BCCDC - Vaccine Management](https://www.bccdc.ca/health-professionals/clinical-resources/vaccine-management) — *cold chain guidance, the biologicals order form, temperature logs and cold chain incident forms; full guidance is in Appendix E of the manual*

## Courses

- [BCCDC - Immunization Courses](https://www.bccdc.ca/health-professionals/education-development/immunization-courses) — *Immunization Competency Course, influenza and COVID-19 courses, communication and vaccine storage courses on the PHSA LearningHub, for providers who work in BC or care for BC patients*

## NP Prescribing Notes

- Check that a drug is within NP prescribing authority in [BCCNM - Nurse Practitioners: Prescribing Drugs](https://www.bccnm.ca/NP/PracticeStandards/Pages/Prescribing.aspx). The full set of NP prescribing rules is on [Practice: Prescribing Authority](../6%20Practice/Prescribing%20Authority.md).
- BCCDC asks providers to check with their regulatory college and employer about requirements for giving immunizations, and to use the most recent version of each part of the manual.
