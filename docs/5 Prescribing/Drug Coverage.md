*How BC PharmaCare covers what you prescribe: Formulary Search, Special Authority, coverage plans and the programs that set what a prescription costs the patient. Coverage criteria for a specific drug are on its condition page.*

## Programs That Set the Price

- [BC PharmaCare programs](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/practitioner-professional-resources/pharmacare/programs)
- [Reference Drug Program (RDP)](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/practitioner-professional-resources/pharmacare/programs/reference-drug-program) — *seven drug classes: at least one reference drug fully covered per class, others partly covered; Special Authority can give full coverage*
- [Low Cost Alternative (LCA) program](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/practitioner-professional-resources/pharmacare/programs/how-pharmacare-covers-drugs-in-the-low-cost-alternative-lca-program) — *for drugs with the same ingredient, strength and form, full coverage up to a price ceiling, usually the generic*
- [Biosimilars Initiative for health professionals](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/practitioner-professional-resources/pharmacare/programs/biosimilars-initiative-health-professionals)
- [Trial Prescription Program](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/practitioner-professional-resources/pharmacare/programs/trial-prescription-program-eligible-drugs)
- [Drug reviews](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/practitioner-professional-resources/pharmacare/health-industry-professionals) — *how PharmaCare decides which new drugs to cover*

## NP Prescribing Notes

- NPs can submit Special Authority requests, including by eForm from private community practice. NP prescribing limits and conditions are set by [BCCNM - Nurse Practitioners: Prescribing Drugs](https://www.bccnm.ca/NP/PracticeStandards/Pages/Prescribing.aspx); PharmaNet and PRIME will sit on Practice: Prescribing authority.
