## Services and Referral

### Coverage and benefits

- [BC PharmaCare - Plan W: Information for health professionals](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/practitioner-professional-resources/pharmacare/plans/plan-w-health-professionals) — *PharmaCare plan for First Nations in BC, funded by FNHA*
- [FNHA - Pharmacy and providers](https://fnha.ca/benefits/benefits-guide/pharmacy-and-providers/) — *Plan W plus the extra pharmacy benefits FNHA administers through Pacific Blue Cross; Health Benefits 1-855-550-5454*
- [FNHA - Health Benefits eligibility](https://fnha.ca/benefits/overview/eligibility/) — *First Nations people with Indian status living in BC*
- [FNHA - Medical transportation](https://fnha.ca/benefits/benefits-guide/medical-transport-ambulance/)
- [FNHA - Mental health benefits](https://fnha.ca/benefits/benefits-guide/mental-health/)
- [Indigenous Services Canada - Non-Insured Health Benefits](https://www.canada.ca/en/indigenous-services-canada/services/non-insured-health-benefits-first-nations-inuit.html) — *federal benefits for eligible First Nations and Inuit not covered by FNHA*
- [Indigenous Services Canada - Jordan's Principle](https://www.canada.ca/en/indigenous-services-canada/services/jordans-principle.html) — *funding for products, services and supports for First Nations children*

### FNHA provincial and virtual services

- [First Nations Virtual Substance Use and Psychiatry Service](https://fnha.ca/services-and-support/access-and-support/health-and-virtual-services/virtual-substance-use-and-psychiatry-service/) — *referral-based addictions medicine and psychiatry for First Nations people and families in BC; NPs can refer; providers call 1-833-456-7655*
- [First Nations Virtual Doctor of the Day](https://fnha.ca/services-and-support/access-and-support/health-and-virtual-services/virtual-doctor-of-the-day/) — *virtual primary care for First Nations people in BC who can't reach their own provider; 1-855-344-3800*
- [Maternity and Babies Advice Line (MaBAL)](https://fnha.ca/services-and-support/access-and-support/health-and-virtual-services/maternity-and-babies-advice-line/) — *24/7 advice for providers in rural and remote communities on pregnancy, birth and newborn care; run by RCCbc with FNHA*
- [First Nations-led Primary Health Care Centres](https://fnha.ca/primary-care-centres/) — *list of centres by region*
- [FNHA - Treatment centres](https://fnha.ca/services-and-support/access-and-support/health-and-virtual-services/treatment-centres/)
- [FNHA - Traditional wellness and healing](https://fnha.ca/services-and-support/culturally-grounded-care/traditional-wellness-and-healing/)

### By region

Each FNHA region page lists its Nations, communities and regional services. Health authority Indigenous health programs include Indigenous patient navigators or liaisons in hospital and community care. Use the [FNHA regional service finder](https://fnha.ca/about/regions/map-and-regions/regional-service-finder/) for local services, and search [Pathways](https://pathwaysbc.ca/) for "Indigenous" to find local liaison and navigator contacts.
