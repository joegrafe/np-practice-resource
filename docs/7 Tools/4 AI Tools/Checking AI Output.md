*NPPR is built so an NP can check what an AI tool says against the source document, as the human in the loop. This page maps each step of the visit to the NPPR section that holds the sources for it, then lists the published guidance on reviewing AI output before it is used or charted.*

## Where to Check Each Step of the Visit

| Step of the visit | What the AI produced | Check it against |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assessment | History questions, exam, scores, tests to order | [Assessments](../../2%20Assessments/index.md) |
| Diagnosis | A differential or a working diagnosis | [Approaches](../../1%20Approaches/index.md) and [Conditions](../../3%20Conditions/index.md) |
| Treatment and prescribing | A management plan, a drug and dose | The condition page's Key Sources and Prescribing section, and [Prescribing](../../5%20Prescribing/index.md) |
| Referral | Where to refer, or whether to | The condition page's Advice and Referral section, and [Populations](../../4%20Populations/index.md) |
| Patient education | A handout or instructions | The condition page's Patient Resources |
| Charting | The visit note | [Documentation, Privacy and Records](../../6%20Practice/Documentation%20Privacy%20and%20Records.md) and the sources below |
