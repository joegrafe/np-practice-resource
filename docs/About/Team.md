## How reviews work

NPPR is built by its editor and checked by clinicians who review pages in their own area of practice. A reviewer checks that a page links to the right, current sources: each source opened, versions and years correct, BC sources first, nothing missing that an NP in that area would expect, and no clinical advice in NPPR's own words. Reviewers don't endorse what the linked guidelines say, and the [disclaimer](Disclaimer%2C%20Licence%20and%20Privacy.md) applies to every page.

Each reviewed page names its reviewers under its title, linked to their card here.

## Join the team

NPPR welcomes registered clinicians (nurse practitioners, physicians, pharmacists and other regulated health professionals) as **reviewers** for pages in their area of practice, and anyone who wants to help as a **contributor**: suggesting links, testing the site, or writing a template. [Contact NPPR](Contact.md) to ask about joining.

Nobody is listed here without their written consent, a photo is optional, and you can ask to be removed at any time. [Privacy](Disclaimer%2C%20Licence%20and%20Privacy.md#team-members) says what NPPR publishes about the team.
