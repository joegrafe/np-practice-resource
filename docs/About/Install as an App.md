NPPR can be added to your phone's home screen, so it opens full screen with one tap, like an app. It still needs an internet connection: pages and the guidelines they link to load from the web.

## iPhone (Safari)

1. Open [nppr.ca](https://www.nppr.ca) in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**. The NPPR icon appears on your home screen.

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open [nppr.ca](https://www.nppr.ca) in Chrome.
2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right corner.
3. Tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**, then **Install**.

Once installed on Android, NPPR also appears in your phone's **Share** menu. Share any web page to NPPR to [suggest it as a link](Submissions.md), with the address already filled in.

## Why install it

- **One tap.** Open NPPR mid-visit without typing an address or finding a bookmark.
- **More room.** The app hides the browser's address bar, leaving more of the screen for the page.
