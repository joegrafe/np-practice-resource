## Disclaimer

NPPR is a reference index for licensed clinicians and students working under supervision. It is not for patients.

- NPPR links to guidance written by other organizations. It doesn't give clinical recommendations of its own, and it doesn't replace your clinical judgement or the source document.
- Always read the linked source. A source can change after the page's last review, and the publisher's current version is the one that counts.
- If you use an AI tool, check its answer against the cited source before acting on it.
- Practise within your scope and your regulator's standards: in BC, the [BCCNM standards for nurse practitioners](https://www.bccnm.ca/NP/PracticeStandards/Pages/Default.aspx).

## Licence

NPPR's own text is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). You may share and adapt it, including for your own practice resource, as long as you credit NPPR.ca.

Linked guidelines, PDFs, logos and tools belong to their publishers and are not covered by this licence. NPPR links to the publisher's own copy and doesn't host third-party documents.

## Access labels

Some sources need an account. NPPR marks them so you know before you click:

- *members only*: free to members of an association, such as the NNPBC NP Portal.
- *login*: free, but needs an account, such as Pathways BC.
- *subscription*: paid, such as RxFiles, UpToDate or Lexicomp. Your health authority library may give free access.

## Privacy

- **No tracking.** NPPR runs no analytics or advertising and sets no tracking cookies of its own. Fonts are served from nppr.ca, not Google.
- **Forms.** The [Submit a link](Submissions.md) and [Contact](Contact.md) forms run on [Tally](https://tally.so/help/privacy-policy). They collect only what you type, plus the page you came from. On the Contact form, your name and email are optional.
- **Searches that find nothing.** When a search on NPPR finds nothing, or finds only some of its words, the words you typed are sent to NPPR with the date, which search box you used and any filters you chose, so the missing topic can be added. Nothing about you is kept: no IP address, account, cookie or device details. Long numbers and email addresses are removed before the search is saved. Other searches aren't sent anywhere.
- **Saved in your browser.** Your last few searches are kept in your browser's storage, on your device only. The optional AI search downloads its language model from Hugging Face the first time you use it, and keeps its search index in your browser's storage too; AI searches aren't sent to NPPR.
- **Never share patient information** in a search, a form or a GitHub issue.
